### 📌 Phase 1 – BYOK AI-Powered MVP

*This phase focuses on establishing the core loop: user configures AI, AI explains a concept, AI generates a challenge.*

### 🎯 **User Story 1: Initial AI Setup (The "First-Run" Experience)**
- **Given** a new user, Alice, starts the application for the first time,
- **When** the app launches,
- **Then** she shall be presented with a mandatory setup screen to enter her AI Provider (e.g., OpenAI), Model (e.g., `gpt-4o`), and API Key, and the system shall validate her credentials before allowing her to proceed.

### 🎯 **User Story 2: AI-Powered Exploration**
- **Given** Alice has successfully configured her API key,
- **When** she selects the concept `Binary Trees` from the knowledge map,
- **Then** the Catalyst Agent shall use her configured LLM to generate a clear explanation based on the source Markdown content.

### 🎯 **User Story 3: Answering an AI-Generated Challenge**
- **Given** Alice has just read the AI-generated explanation of `Binary Trees`,
- **When** the system presents a challenge,
- **Then** that challenge shall be dynamically generated by her LLM, and her answer shall be evaluated for correctness by the system (potentially with AI assistance).

### 🎯 **User Story 4: Saving Progress**
- **Given** Alice has answered 5 AI-generated questions,
- **When** she enters the command `checkpoint save`,
- **Then** the system shall save her progress, including which concepts she has covered and her performance statistics, into a checkpoint file.

### 📌 Phase 2 – Gamified Progression & Analytics

*This phase builds on the AI core, adding robust feedback mechanisms, user controls, and smarter progression.*

### 🎯 **User Story 1: AI-Assisted Adaptive Difficulty**
- **Given** Bob's performance history shows he struggles with "Sorting Algorithms",
- **When** he incorrectly answers another AI-generated question about `Merge Sort`,
- **Then** the Assessment Engine shall flag the concept as a weak point, and the Catalyst Agent shall prompt the LLM to generate an easier, more foundational follow-up question.

### 🎯 **User Story 2: Viewing Actionable Analytics**
- **Given** Bob has studied for 3 days using his configured AI,
- **When** he views his stats,
- **Then** the system shall display an analytics dashboard showing proficiency trends (e.g., "Sorting Algorithms" accuracy dropped from 70% → 45%) and highlight his strengths ("Data Structures").

### 🎯 **User Story 3: Changing the AI Model**
- **Given** Bob wants to switch from a powerful but expensive model to a faster, cheaper one for practice,
- **When** he navigates to preferences and changes his model from `gpt-4o` to `claude-3-haiku`,
- **Then** the system shall validate the new configuration, and all subsequent explanations and challenges shall be generated using the newly selected model.

### 🎯 **User Story 4: Autosave**
- **Given** Bob is in the middle of a session,
- **When** the application closes unexpectedly,
- **Then** upon restart, the system shall restore him to his last known state, including the concept he was working on and his recent Q&A history.

### 📌 Phase 3 – Autonomous Tutor

*This phase elevates the AI from a tool to a proactive partner in learning, capable of leading conversations and guiding the user's path.*

### 🎯 **User Story 1: Deeply Personalized Explanations**
- **Given** the system's analysis of Carol's past AI-evaluated answers shows she struggles with mathematical notation,
- **When** she explores "Gradient Descent",
- **Then** the Catalyst Agent shall instruct her LLM to generate an explanation using analogies and code examples, explicitly referencing her previous confusion with "Big O notation" to bridge the gap.

### 🎯 **User Story 2: Semantic Evaluation of Open-Ended Answers**
- **Given** Carol is asked the open-ended question, “Explain why a high learning rate can be a problem,”
- **When** she writes, “The algorithm might get jumpy and bounce around, never finding the bottom,”
- **Then** the LLM shall semantically evaluate the answer as correct, recognizing the meaning behind "jumpy" and "bounce around," and provide positive feedback.

### 🎯 **User Story 3: Navigator Mode for Free-Form Inquiry**
- **Given** Carol wants to ask a question outside the current learning path,
- **When** she enters the query, `How is a 'heap' different from a 'binary search tree'?`,
- **Then** the Catalyst Agent shall use her LLM to generate a comparative answer, pulling context from relevant sections of the source material.

### 🎯 **User Story 4: Proactive AI-Driven Guidance**
- **Given** Carol has successfully mastered several foundational data structure concepts,
- **When** she finishes the "Queues" topic,
- **Then** the Catalyst Agent shall proactively suggest, "You've got a great handle on linear data structures. Based on your profile, I recommend we tackle 'Tree Traversal Algorithms' next to build on that. Shall we begin?"
